STEP 03 // AUTOMATIC FAIL-SAFE RELEASE

Escalation executes only when silence is permanent

If you miss a scheduled check-in, the switch transitions to WARNED and alerts you across multiple channels. If silence continues past the GRACE window, single-use cryptographically signed release tokens are dispatched to your designated recipients to unlock only what you assigned to them.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions & answers.

Everything you need to know about fail-safe reliability and cryptographic safety.

What if I am traveling or forget to check in?

You are never abruptly compromised. When your check-in deadline passes, LastWord enters the WARNED state and contacts you repeatedly across your configured channels. If you still don't respond, the GRACE period activates. Your switch is only released after all escalation stages expire.

Can LastWord or AWS read my vault contents?

No. All notes and files uploaded to LastWord are encrypted with envelope KMS keys. Only holders of the generated release tokens can decrypt and access assigned items.

How do my beneficiaries access my vault?

When the switch is released, your beneficiaries receive a cryptographically signed, expiring access token. They do not need a LastWord account—they click their secure link to download and decrypt their designated assets.
